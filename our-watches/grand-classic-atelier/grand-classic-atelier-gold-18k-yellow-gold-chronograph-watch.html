Grand Classic Atelier Gold, an 18K Yellow-Gold Chronograph Watch

Ref. GCYG11CHM · 18K yellow gold · black 18K gold dial, hand guilloché
Also in 18K white gold

The MHP Grand Classic Atelier Gold is a monopusher chronograph watch from MHP, an independent French watch brand founded in Normandy. It comes in 18K yellow gold, in a limited edition of 10 pieces. Its black dial is guillochéd by hand, and every artisan who worked on it is named. It is made for collectors who value rare, traceable craft over a famous logo.

Design and inspiration

The design comes from MHP's roots in Normandy and the pioneers of early aviation. Pilots once timed flights with a single button. The Grand Classic Atelier keeps that idea: the chronograph pusher sits inside the crown, so the 41 mm case stays clean and symmetrical. Its signatures are easy to spot: a large aviation crown, a concave bezel and lugs shaped by electro-erosion. Two counters, openworked gold hands and a domed sapphire crystal give the black dial depth. The case is 41 mm wide and 13 mm thick, with a sapphire caseback that shows the hand-finished movement.

The hand-guilloché dial and the movement

The 18K gold dial is black rhodium-plated, then engraved by hand on guilloché machines 50 to 100 years old. The "grains de riz" pattern takes 6 to 10 hours per dial. Hand guilloché leaves small variations, so no two dials are alike. The mechanical calibre GCS01CHM beats at 28,800 vibrations per hour and stores a 60-hour power reserve. The movement is finished by hand. The case is water-resistant to 10 ATM and closes on a black alligator strap with a custom deployant clasp. Each piece is assembled by hand by a small team of named artisans, not on a production line.

Your questions, answered

What is the MHP Grand Classic Atelier Gold?

A limited-edition chronograph watch from MHP, an independent French watch brand: 41 mm 18K yellow-gold case, hand-guilloché 18K gold dial, manual monopusher chronograph. Only 10 pieces.

How does a monopusher chronograph work?

One single pusher controls it: press once to start, again to stop, a third time to reset. Here: manual calibre MHP GCS01CHM, 4 Hz, 60-hour power reserve.

What is a hand-guilloché dial?

A dial engraved by hand, line by line, on guilloché machines 50 to 100 years old. The "grains de riz" pattern takes 6 to 10 hours per dial. Here in 18K gold, black rhodium-plated.

Can I see the watch before I buy it?

Yes. You can book a private viewing at the Paris showroom, 30 Rue Danielle Casanova, or at the workshop in Normandy, by appointment.

How many pieces are made?

The yellow-gold Grand Classic Atelier Gold is limited to ten pieces.
